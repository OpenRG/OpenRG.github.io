Open Research Group


On Wednesday, September 9, 2026, the board of Open Research Group voted unanimously to dissolve and liquidate the company. Founded June 2016, OpenRG operated for more than 10 years as an advocate and agent for developing and implementing open source policy models for transparent and replicable analysis. Our goal was to democratize the policy process. We will continue that goal through the other organizations with which we participate.

Thanks for 10 great years. And thanks to all the expert collaborators who joined us in this goal. Keep up the good work.

Sincerely,

Richard W. Evans, PhD

President and Co-founder
